# ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES

## HIR
- Validate intent against PSC and governance constraints.
- Create FIS draft and ticketed decisions.

## PL
- Approve/reject FIS and scope expansions.

## AA
- Produce architecture, contract, and feature specs.

## DEV
- Implement only approved bolt scope.

## QA
- Author and run governed tests for acceptance criteria.

## OPS
- Validate runtime safety and operational readiness.

## DOC
- Produce release and governance documentation.

## DME
- Maintain traceability chain and drift analysis.

## CCS
- Block unsafe or non-compliant transitions.

## AISA
- Execute brownfield migration and cleanup under governance.
